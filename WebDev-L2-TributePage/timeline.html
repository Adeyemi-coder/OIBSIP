Selected milestones

A selective chronology, not a complete life record.

  1. 1934

    Born in Abeokuta

    Akinwande Oluwole Babatunde Soyinka is born on 13 July in western Nigeria.

  2. 1958

    The Royal Court Theatre

    After Leeds, he works as a dramaturg at London’s Royal Court Theatre. His first plays are staged in Ibadan.

  3. 1960

    Home for independence

    He returns on a Rockefeller bursary, founds The 1960 Masks and writes A Dance of the Forests.

  4. 1967

    Arrested during the civil war

    After appealing for a cease-fire, he is accused of conspiring with the Biafran side and detained.

  5. 1969

    Released after 22 months

    The experience becomes The Man Died and A Shuttle in the Crypt.

  6. 1975

    Back at Ife

    He returns to Nigeria and becomes professor of comparative literature at the University of Ife.

  7. 1986

    Nobel Prize in Literature

    He becomes the first African to win the prize. His lecture is dedicated to Nelson Mandela.

  8. 1994

    Exile under Abacha

    He leaves Nigeria under General Sani Abacha’s military rule. In 1997 the regime charges him with treason.

  9. 1998

    Return to Nigeria

    He comes home after Abacha’s death, ending four years in exile.

  10. 2004

    The Reith Lectures

    He delivers the BBC Reith Lectures, later published as Climate of Fear.

  11. 2024

    Honoured at ninety

    Lagos’s National Theatre will be renamed the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts.
