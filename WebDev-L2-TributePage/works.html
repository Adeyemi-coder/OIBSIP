02Selected works

A working index

Plays, novels, poetry and memoir, from 1959 to 2021.

15 works

  • 1959

    The Lion and the Jewel

    A comedy that sets a Westernised schoolteacher against a village chief, and old ways against new.

    Play
  • 1960

    A Dance of the Forests

    Written for Nigeria’s independence celebrations.

    Play
  • 1960

    The Trials of Brother Jero

    A satire on the opportunistic preachers of upstart prayer churches.

    Play
  • 1965

    The Road

    A philosophical play among drivers and drifters at a roadside, preoccupied with death and meaning.

    Play
  • 1965

    Kongi’s Harvest

    A satire of an authoritarian ruler and the festival built around him.

    Play
  • 1965

    The Interpreters

    A novel following a circle of young Nigerian intellectuals as they debate life after independence.

    Novel
  • 1967

    Idanre and Other Poems

    Poems rooted in Yoruba myth, with the god Ogun at the centre of the title poem.

    Poetry
  • 1970

    Madmen and Specialists

    A dark satire that followed the civil war years.

    Play
  • 1972

    The Man Died: Prison Notes

    His account of 22 months of detention during the civil war.

    Memoir
  • 1972

    A Shuttle in the Crypt

    A collection that includes poetry written during imprisonment.

    Poetry
  • 1973

    Season of Anomy

    A novel shaped by his thoughts in prison, setting the myth of Orpheus and Eurydice against Yoruba mythology.

    Novel
  • 1976

    Death and the King’s Horseman

    Set in colonial Oyo: a king’s horseman, ritual duty, and a British district officer who tries to stop it. Published 1975, first staged 1976.

    Play
  • 1981

    Aké: The Years of Childhood

    A memoir of his childhood in Abeokuta.

    Memoir
  • 2006

    You Must Set Forth at Dawn

    A memoir of the years of exile and the Abacha dictatorship.

    Memoir
  • 2021

    Chronicles from the Land of the Happiest People on Earth

    A satirical whodunit set in an imaginary Nigeria, and his first novel in 48 years.

    Novel

Plays are dated by first performance; other works by first publication.
