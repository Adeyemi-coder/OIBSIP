    How it works

    Calc evaluates operations in the order you enter them, like a desk calculator: 5 + 3 × 2 gives 16. Percent works the way a shop till does: 200 + 10 % gives 220. MC, MR, M+ and M− keep a number in memory. Sci follows true order of operations with brackets, powers and square roots: 5 + 3 × 2 gives 11.

    Press equals and the full equation sprouts from the ground behind the calculator, then settles into the scene. Older equations grow fainter and drift deeper. Calm mode hides them. The History list holds the same record as text, is kept on this device, and lets you tap a result to reuse it.

    Nursing has twelve calculators, from dosage and drip rates to MAP and Parkland, each with sanity checks, plus a searchable library of forty formulas. Convert switches between weights, volumes, temperatures, lengths and times. Add Verdant to your home screen and it also works offline.

    The tools are a study aid. Always check calculations against your facility’s protocols, and have high-alert medications verified by a second nurse or your pharmacist.
